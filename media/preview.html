How to Use

Add your photos to the images/ folder with these exact filenames:

  • hero-bg.jpg - Main hero background (1920x1080px)
  • story-1.jpg - First story card (800x600px)
  • story-2.jpg - Second story card (800x600px)
  • story-3.jpg - Third story card (800x600px)
  • venue.jpg - Venue photo (1200x800px)
  • gallery-1.jpg to gallery-4.jpg - Gallery photos (800x800px)

Then refresh this page to see your images!
